Wills in Nigeria Your will needs two witnesses, present at the same time.
The formalities For a will to be valid:
01It is in writing
02You sign it, or someone signs
for you, in your presence and
at your direction
03You sign or acknowledge it
before at least two witnesses,
present at the same time
04The witnesses sign it
in your presence
What the law says The will must be signed or acknowledged before two or more witnesses, present at the same time. Wills Law of Lagos State · S.4 Wills Act 1837 · S.9
Watch out Never let a beneficiary witness your will. If they or their spouse do, the gift to them is void, unless two other witnesses also signed the will. Either way, the rest of the will stands. Wills Law of Lagos State · S.8
Get it wrong, and the will can fail.
Your estate may then be shared as if you left no will at all.
